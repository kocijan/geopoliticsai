Methodology & Legal Distinctions

These three initiatives differ substantially in legal form and intent:

1. WAICO (World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization)

An intergovernmental treaty organization established in Shanghai on 16 July 2026, oriented toward the Global South and open-weight AI architectures.

2. Pax Silica

A U.S. State Department-led economic-security framework established in December 2025 focusing on semiconductor fabrication, critical minerals refining, and compute supply-chain resilience.

3. Call for Control of Frontier AI Models — "Frontier Control" (21 September 2026)

An open political declaration launched on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly by Finnish President Alexander Stubb and Norwegian Prime Minister Jonas Gahr Støre, endorsed by 28 leaders and senior officials representing 26 countries and the European Commission (including recent accessions from Austria, Romania, Liechtenstein, Luxembourg, France, and Sierra Leone). It calls for mandatory pre-deployment safety testing, independent third-party evaluations, incident reporting, and exploring a UN-style international oversight institution.

European Union Representation

The European Commission signed Pax Silica on 23 June 2026, and President Ursula von der Leyen endorsed the Frontier Control declaration. EU member states are represented via the EU institutional signature. On the map, EU-covered states are shown with a hatched Pax fill; direct national signatories (e.g. Germany, Greece, Netherlands, Italy) retain a solid fill.

Pax Silica Status Taxonomy

This tracker distinguishes: formal signatories (signed the Pax Silica Declaration nationally), EU-represented states (covered via the European Commission's institutional signature), observers (recognized observer participation), non-signatory participants (endorsed principles via separate instruments, e.g. Taiwan), and AI Opportunity Statement signatories (signed the related but distinct Joint Statement on AI Opportunity, e.g. Portugal).
