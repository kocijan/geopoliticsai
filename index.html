Methodology & Legal Distinctions

These three initiatives differ substantially in legal form and intent:

1. WAICO (World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization)

An intergovernmental treaty organization established in Shanghai on 16 July 2026, oriented toward the Global South and open-weight AI architectures.

2. Pax Silica

A U.S. State Department-led economic-security framework established in December 2025 focusing on semiconductor fabrication, critical minerals refining, and compute supply-chain resilience.

3. Call for Control of Frontier AI Models (21 September 2026)

An open political declaration launched on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly by Finnish President Alexander Stubb and Norwegian Prime Minister Jonas Gahr Støre, endorsed by 22 world leaders and the European Commission. It calls for mandatory testing, independent evaluations, and exploring international oversight.

European Union Representation

The European Union signed Pax Silica on 23 June 2026, and European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen endorsed the Frontier AI Call. On the map, EU member states are colored into Pax Silica, while the country details window and register distinguish direct national signatories from states covered via EU accession.
